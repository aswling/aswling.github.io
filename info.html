Alex Ling is a designer, comics artist, and printmaker based in New York. She’s currently a Senior Brand Designer at Etsy, where she works alongside creatives and designers to make brand experiences with human connection at the forefront. Previous clients include the Asian American Arts Alliance, Starbucks, Maev, Google Maps, and Chase.

Creatively, Alex is interested in themes of belonging and our connectedness with the natural world, and explores these ideas through print media. When she’s not at her desk, she’s usually found biking, knitting, or catching movies at IFC.
